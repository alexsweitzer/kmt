Abandoned boat removal

Safe, efficient removal and disposal of abandoned, derelict, or sunken boats across Maryland, Virginia, and Delaware.

Request a quoteGovernment projects

We specialize in the safe, efficient removal and disposal of abandoned, derelict, or sunken boats from public waterways, coastal areas, and private properties across Maryland, Virginia, and Delaware. Our experienced marine recovery team partners with local, state, and federal agencies to keep waterways navigable and protect the environment from the hazards of abandoned vessels.

Governments & agencies

We work closely with harbor masters, natural resource departments, environmental agencies, and law enforcement to remove vessels in full compliance with all applicable state and federal abandoned boat programs.

Our team understands the unique requirements of government-funded removal projects, including:

  • Adherence to Maryland’s Abandoned Boat and Debris Program (DNR)
  • Compliance with Virginia’s Derelict Vessel Act
  • Coordination with Delaware’s Division of Fish & Wildlife
  • Documentation, evidence collection, and reporting for environmental and legal records
  • Environmentally responsible decontamination, fuel removal, and recycling

We manage every stage, from site assessment and permitting to towing, transport, and certified disposal, ensuring accountability and transparency for government contracts.

Private property & marina boat removal

If an abandoned or unwanted boat is left on your dock, marina, or private land, we can help. Whether it’s a small fishing boat, large yacht, or partially submerged vessel, we have the equipment and experience to safely remove and dispose of it. Our team handles:

  • Derelict boats left by former tenants
  • Storm-damaged or sunken vessels
  • Unwanted or unregistered watercraft
  • Removal from backyards, beaches, slips, and tidal areas

We’ll ensure the removal process is safe, efficient, and fully compliant with state and environmental regulations.
