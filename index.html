Our service area

Kentmorr Marine Transport provides boat towing services throughout the Mid-Atlantic. Our on-water marine service coverage is within one hour from our headquarters in Stevensville, Maryland, in the heart of the Chesapeake Bay region. Our trucking division runs up and down the East Coast and out to the Midwest.
