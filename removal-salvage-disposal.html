By water

If your boat is in trouble, call us to the rescue! Whether you need emergency help or recovery after a storm, we've got you covered. Our experienced captains can navigate the most challenging waters to recover your boat.

Need help with an abandoned boat?

We can help with that too. We work with private individuals and government agencies to remove boats that impede waterway safety.

By land

We have all the equipment needed to safely remove a distressed boat on land. Our team can tow your boat to our marina for repairs, or we can tow it for recycling and disposal if necessary.

We also remove abandoned boats from private and public property. If there is an abandoned boat causing an eyesore or safety issue, our team can help.
