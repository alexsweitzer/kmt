We live and breathe boats

When you hire us, you aren’t just getting someone to hold the steering wheel.

Request a quote

Our captains and drivers live and breathe boats. We’ve spent decades (and in some cases our whole lives!) navigating the Chesapeake Bay and its tributaries.

Kentmorr Marine Transport was founded to fix an ugly problem we saw far too often.

Boat owners would trust the wrong people with transporting their boats, resulting in damage and safety risks.

We take the fear out of moving your boat.

We assess each unique situation and take the steps necessary to get the job done efficiently and safely.

We show up on time & prepared

When you schedule the date and time for your move, we show up on time, every time. We also plan enough time for your move so that we never have to rush or cut corners. We make sure the boat is positioned correctly, strapped down appropriately, and all on-board components are secured and stowed away.

Our vehicles carry extras of everything.

Each truck carries a minimum of 20 ratchets, 4 oversize load banners, a clean stock of padding, and a vast assortment of tools to handle any situation. Trucks are outfitted with onboard air to alleviate downtime should a tire fail in transit.

Our tow boat is just as well prepared.

It carries extra diesel and gasoline, spare dock lines, spare tow lines, extra life preservers, extra flares, multiple bilge/trash pumps, and a wide variety of tools onboard.

We are professionals

We do things the right way and the legal way.

We are fully insured, and our drivers and captains are all properly licensed and experienced. Our drivers abide by all DOT regulations, and each truck is outfitted with an OBD ELD (electronic logging device) with front- and rear-facing cameras.

Our equipment is kept in top condition.

Your boat will not be secured with a pile of mismatched ratchet straps and padding from the hardware store. Your boat will not arrive covered in mud with non-functional lights, or with fluids leaking everywhere.
